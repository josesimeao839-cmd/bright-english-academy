Lesson 2

The Alphabet & Spelling

Learn the English alphabet and how to spell simple words.

🎯 Lesson Objectives

By the end of this lesson, you will be able to:

  • Recognize the 26 letters of the English alphabet.
  • Say the letters correctly.
  • Ask someone to spell a word.
  • Spell your name in English.
  • Understand simple spelling questions.
  • Spell basic English words.

🔊 2. Pronunciation

Pay special attention to these letters.

A → ei
E → ii
I → ai
O → ou
U → you
H → eitch
W → double-you
Y → wai
💡 Practice: Say each letter slowly and clearly.

🧠 5. Grammar — This & That

We use this for something near us and that for something farther away.

Word Meaning Example
This Isto / Este / Esta This is a book.
That Isso / Aquele / Aquela That is a school.
This is my name.
Este é o meu nome.
That is my book.
Aquele é o meu livro.
Remember:

This → near
That → far

🗣️ 6. Dialogue

Teacher: What's your name?

Student: My name is David.

Teacher: How do you spell your name?

Student: D-A-V-I-D.

Teacher: Very good!

Student: Thank you.

Teacher: Can you spell "English"?

Student: E-N-G-L-I-S-H.

Teacher: Excellent!

Portuguese Translation

Professor: Qual é o seu nome?

Aluno: Meu nome é David.

Professor: Como se soletra o seu nome?

Aluno: D-A-V-I-D.

Professor: Muito bem!

Aluno: Obrigado.

Professor: Pode soletrar "English"?

Aluno: E-N-G-L-I-S-H.

Professor: Excelente!

📖 7. Reading

Hello! My name is Ana.

I am a student at Bright English Academy.

I am learning English.

My teacher asks me to spell my name.

I say: A-N-A.

Then I spell the word "English": E-N-G-L-I-S-H.

I enjoy learning English every day.

Reading Questions

  1. What is her name?
  2. Where does she study?
  3. How does she spell her name?
  4. What word does she spell?
Answers:

1. Her name is Ana.

2. She studies at Bright English Academy.

3. A-N-A.

4. She spells the word "English".

✍️ 8. Writing Practice

Write your name and spell it in English.

My name is __________________.

I spell it: __________________________.

My favorite English word is: __________________________.

I spell it: __________________________.

Try to write at least five sentences using words from this lesson.

🗣️ 9. Speaking Practice

Practice this conversation with a partner.

A: What's your name?

B: My name is __________.

A: How do you spell it?

B: It's __________.

A: Thank you.

B: You're welcome.

💡 Practice the conversation several times. Then try it without reading.
