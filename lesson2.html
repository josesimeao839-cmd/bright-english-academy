Lesson 2

The Alphabet

Learn the 26 letters of the English alphabet and practice their pronunciation.

🎯 Lesson Objectives

By the end of this lesson, you will be able to:

  • Recognize the 26 letters of the English alphabet.
  • Say the letters correctly.
  • Spell simple words.
  • Ask someone to spell a word.
  • Give your name letter by letter.

🔊 2. Letter Pronunciation

Read each letter aloud.

A → ay
B → bee
C → see
D → dee
E → ee
G → jee
H → aitch
I → eye
J → jay
U → you
W → double-you
Y → why
Z → zee
Important:

In American English, Z is commonly pronounced "zee".

In British English, Z is commonly pronounced "zed".

✏️ 3. Spelling

Spelling means saying the letters of a word one by one.

CAT

C → A → T

see → ay → tee
BOOK

B → O → O → K

bee → oh → oh → kay
SCHOOL

S → C → H → O → O → L

💡 Tip: Spell your name aloud every day.

💬 4. Useful Expressions

How do you spell your name?
Como se soletra o seu nome?
How do you spell it?
Como se soletra isso?
My name is José.
Meu nome é José.
J-O-S-É.
As letras do nome, uma por uma.
Can you spell that, please?
Pode soletrar isso, por favor?

🔠 5. Capital Letters and Lowercase Letters

English letters can be written in uppercase or lowercase.

Uppercase Lowercase Example
A a Apple
B b Book
C c Cat
D d Dog
E e English
Remember:

We normally use a capital letter at the beginning of a sentence and for names of people and places.

Example: José is from Mozambique.

🗣️ 6. Dialogue

Teacher: What's your name?

Student: My name is David.

Teacher: How do you spell your name?

Student: D-A-V-I-D.

Teacher: Very good!

Student: Thank you.

📖 7. Reading
