📚 About the Academy

Welcome to Bright English Academy .

Bright English Academy is an English learning project created to help learners develop practical English communication skills.

Our lessons are designed to be simple, organized, practical and engaging, allowing learners to study English step by step.

👨‍🏫 Founder & Instructor

José Vilanculos

Founder and instructor of Bright English Academy .

The academy was created with the goal of providing learners with a clear and practical way to develop their English skills.

🎯 Our Mission

Our mission is to help learners build confidence in English through practical, accessible and engaging learning experiences.

We focus on communication, vocabulary, grammar, pronunciation, reading, writing and speaking.

🌟 Our Vision

Our vision is to create a learning environment where students can develop their English skills step by step and communicate with greater confidence.

🌱 Beginner A1

Our Beginner A1 course is designed for learners who are starting their English journey.

Students learn essential English through structured lessons covering greetings, introductions, vocabulary, grammar, numbers, colors, dates and everyday communication.
