Lesson 1

Greetings & Introductions

Learn how to greet people and introduce yourself in English.

🎯 Lesson Objectives

By the end of this lesson, you will be able to:

  • Greet people in English.
  • Say goodbye politely.
  • Introduce yourself.
  • Ask someone's name.
  • Ask and answer "How are you?"
  • Use basic personal information.

📚 1. Vocabulary

English Portuguese Example
Hello Olá Hello, Maria!
Hi Oi Hi, John!
Good morning Bom dia Good morning, teacher.
Good afternoon Boa tarde Good afternoon!
Good evening Boa noite Good evening, Mr. John.
Goodbye Adeus / Até logo Goodbye, Mary.
See you later Até logo See you later!
Thank you Obrigado(a) Thank you very much.
Please Por favor Please, sit down.
Nice to meet you Prazer em conhecê-lo(a) Nice to meet you!

🔊 2. Pronunciation

Practice saying these expressions aloud.

Hello → heh-LOH
Hi → hai
Good morning → gud MOR-ning
Good afternoon → gud af-ter-NOON
Good evening → gud EEV-ning
Goodbye → gud-BYE
Thank you → THANK yoo
Nice to meet you → nais tuh MEET yoo
💡 Tip: Don't worry about being perfect. Repeat each expression several times and try to speak clearly.

🧠 4. Grammar — Verb To Be

The verb to be is one of the most important verbs in English.

Affirmative Form

Subject Verb Example Portuguese
I am I am José. Eu sou José.
You are You are a student. Você é estudante.
He is He is John. Ele é John.
She is She is Maria. Ela é Maria.
We are We are students. Nós somos estudantes.
They are They are teachers. Eles/Elas são professores.

Short Forms

I am → I'm
Eu sou / estou.
You are → You're
Você é / está.
He is → He's
Ele é / está.
She is → She's
Ela é / está.
We are → We're
Nós somos / estamos.
They are → They're
Eles/Elas são / estão.
Remember:

I → am
You → are
He → is
She → is
We → are
They → are

🗣️ 5. Dialogue

John: Good morning!

Maria: Good morning!

John: What's your name?

Maria: My name is Maria. What's your name?

John: I'm John. Nice to meet you.

Maria: Nice to meet you too.

John: How are you?

Maria: I'm fine, thank you. And you?

John: I'm very well, thank you.

Portuguese Translation

John: Bom dia!

Maria: Bom dia!

John: Qual é o seu nome?

Maria: Meu nome é Maria. Qual é o seu nome?

John: Eu sou John. Prazer em conhecê-la.

Maria: Prazer em conhecê-lo também.

John: Como você está?

Maria: Estou bem, obrigada. E você?

John: Estou muito bem, obrigado.

📖 6. Reading

Hello! My name is David.

I am a beginner English student. I am from Mozambique.

I study English at Bright English Academy. My teacher is friendly and helpful.

I like learning English.

Reading Questions

  1. What is his name?
  2. Where is he from?
  3. Where does he study English?
  4. Does he like learning English?
Answers:

1. His name is David.

2. He is from Mozambique.

3. He studies at Bright English Academy.

4. Yes, he does.

✍️ 7. Writing Practice

Write a short introduction about yourself.

Hello! My name is ________.

I am from ________.

I am a ________.

I am ________ years old.

Nice to meet you!

Try to write at least five sentences.

🗣️ 8. Speaking Practice

Practice this conversation with a partner.

A: Hello! What's your name?

B: My name is ________. What's your name?

A: I'm ________. Nice to meet you.

B: Nice to meet you too.

A: How are you?

B: I'm ________, thank you.

💡 Practice the conversation several times. Then try it without reading.
