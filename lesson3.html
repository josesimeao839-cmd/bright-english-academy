Lesson 3

Numbers 0–100

Learn how to read, say and use numbers in English.

🎯 Lesson Objectives

By the end of this lesson, you will be able to:

  • Count from 0 to 100 in English.
  • Recognize numbers when you hear them.
  • Say your age in English.
  • Ask and answer questions about numbers.
  • Use numbers in everyday situations.
  • Write numbers correctly in English.

🔢 2. Numbers 21–100

Numbers from 21 to 99 are usually formed with a tens number + a unit number.

21 = Twenty-one
Vinte e um
35 = Thirty-five
Trinta e cinco
48 = Forty-eight
Quarenta e oito
62 = Sixty-two
Sessenta e dois
79 = Seventy-nine
Setenta e nove
100 = One hundred
Cem

💡 Remember:

We write numbers such as 21, 35 and 48 with a hyphen between the tens and the unit.

🔊 3. Pronunciation

Practice saying these numbers aloud.

One → wun
Two → too
Three → three
Five → faiv
Eight → ayt
Thirteen → thur-TEEN
Twenty → TWEN-tee
Thirty → THUR-tee
Forty → FOR-tee
One hundred → wun HUN-dred
💡 Practice slowly first. Then try to say the numbers faster and naturally.

🧠 4. Grammar & Common Mistakes

Be careful with numbers ending in -teen and numbers ending in -ty.

Thirteen = 13
Treze
Thirty = 30
Trinta
Fourteen = 14
Catorze
Forty = 40
Quarenta
Remember:

13 → thirteen
30 → thirty

14 → fourteen
40 → forty

💬 5. Useful Expressions

How old are you?
Quantos anos você tem?
I am twenty years old.
Eu tenho vinte anos.
How many students are there?
Quantos estudantes há?
There are thirty students.
Há trinta estudantes.
What is your phone number?
Qual é o seu número de telefone?
My phone number is 123456789.
O meu número de telefone é 123456789.

🗣️ 6. Dialogue

John: Hello, Maria!

Maria: Hello, John!

John: How old are you?

Maria: I am twenty years old.

John: How old is your brother?

Maria: He is fifteen years old.

John: How many students are in your class?

Maria: There are thirty students.

Portuguese Translation

John: Olá, Maria!

Maria: Olá, John!

John: Quantos anos você tem?

Maria: Eu tenho vinte anos.

John: Quantos anos tem o seu irmão?

Maria: Ele tem quinze anos.

John: Quantos estudantes há na sua turma?

Maria: Há trinta estudantes.

📖 7. Reading

Hello! My name is David.

I am twenty-one years old. I am from Mozambique.

I have two brothers and one sister.

There are forty students in my English class.

I study English every week.

Reading Questions

  1. How old is David?
  2. Where is David from?
  3. How many brothers does David have?
  4. How many sisters does David have?
  5. How many students are in his English class?
Answers:

1. David is twenty-one years old.

2. He is from Mozambique.

3. He has two brothers.

4. He has one sister.

5. There are forty students.

✍️ 8. Writing Practice

Write sentences using numbers.

My name is ________.

I am ________ years old.

I have ________ brothers.

I have ________ sisters.

There are ________ students in my class.

Try to write at least five complete sentences.

🗣️ 9. Speaking Practice

Practice this conversation with a partner.

A: How old are you?

B: I am ________ years old.

A: How many brothers do you have?

B: I have ________ brothers.

A: How many sisters do you have?

B: I have ________ sisters.

A: How many students are in your class?

B: There are ________ students.

💡 Say the numbers aloud. Try not to read the answers while speaking.
